Every good project starts with a small first step. This post is mine.

I created this blog as a home for ideas that deserve more space than a quick
note or social post. It is where I will document what I build, explain what I
learn, and reflect on the decisions that shape the work.

## What I plan to write about

The exact mix will evolve, but most posts will fall into a few broad themes:

- **Building:** practical notes from projects, experiments, and prototypes.
- **Learning:** concepts explained in my own words, with useful examples.
- **Working:** tools and habits that make thoughtful work easier.
- **Reflecting:** lessons that only become clear after stepping back.

## Why write in public?

Writing is a way to test whether I really understand something. Publishing adds
just enough pressure to make the explanation clearer and the examples more
useful. If a post saves someone else a little time—or sparks a better
question—it has done its job.

Thanks for stopping by. There is more to come.
